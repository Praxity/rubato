# Brief: returns without a receipt

From: the training lead at a regional hardware store chain (fictional).

Our cashiers keep getting no-receipt returns wrong. Some refuse returns they should take, some give cash when the policy says store credit, and some take back items over the limit without calling a manager. Customers complain and we lose money both ways.

Who: part-time cashiers, mostly 17 to 25, many in their first year. They learn on their phones during a paid 10-minute slot at the start of a shift. English only.

What we want them to do: handle a no-receipt return the way the policy says, and call a manager when the policy says to.

The policy in short:
- Under $30 with the item in stock: store credit, no manager.
- $30 to $150: store credit, and the cashier must scan the customer's ID.
- Over $150, or any power tool: call a manager. No exceptions.
- Never cash without a receipt.

What I think gets in the way:
- A no-receipt return comes up maybe twice a month per cashier, so they forget the thresholds.
- Customers push hard, and new cashiers give in.
- The point-of-sale screen still shows last year's limits ($25 and $100) on the returns menu. IT says the fix is "in the queue".

We need something live in three weeks. A short course is fine. We'd like to know who finished it.
